What is the purpose of the log message you supply to your git commit?

The log messages detail what changes you made to a staged file 

What do you think makes a good commit message?

A good commit message should be detailed so that others review your changes can understand the differences in versions easily.

When and how often should I be committing my work?

You should commit whenever you make revisions to the staged file.
